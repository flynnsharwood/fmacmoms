Script J

You must write a script based on the prompt below.

First Teenager (Privileged, Troubled)​

  • Well-dressed in designer clothes
  • Sitting in a plush armchair, visibly tense
  • Bruised from a recent fight (split lip, swollen cheek)
  • Irritable, rebellious, and emotionally isolated despite immense privilege
  • Nursed wounds as a symbol of repeated defiance
  • Believes he can rely on his mother’s approval, though misguided

Second Teenager (Rival)​

  • Also from a wealthy family, but ostentatious in appearance
  • Smug and provocative attitude
  • Nursed wounds from the same fight (black eye, bloodied knuckle)
  • Aggressive posture, arms crossed, staring coldly at the first boy
  • Deep personal rivalry worsened by family conflict
  • His father is trying to buy out the first boy’s father’s company (corporate rivalry background)

School Counselor (Recently Divorced Woman)​

  • Late thirties, weary yet empathetic expression
  • Holds a file but focuses more on the tension than paperwork
  • Recently divorced, carrying quiet sadness
  • Took a job hoping to help troubled youths
  • Frustrated by a system that protects the privileged
  • Feels powerless, guilt over inability to reach either boy

Mother of First Teenager (Corporate Lawyer)​

  • High-powered, cold, prioritizes family reputation and success
  • Dismissive of the son’s needs and emotions
  • Sees the boy as a failure, focused solely on appearances and achievement

Father of First Teenager (CEO with Humble Background)​

  • Quietly considerate but always busy
  • Runs a multinational company built from humble origins
  • Guilt-ridden, thinks about his son often, but never shows it
  • Emotionally distant due to work and societal expectations
  • Son misinterprets this as an absence of care
